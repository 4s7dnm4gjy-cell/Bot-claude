## Description (Etsy et Gumroad)

Spend less time planning and more time coaching.

This pack gives you 150 ready-to-use AI prompts written by a track and field athlete, for coaches at every level. Copy a prompt, fill in the brackets with your athletes' details, and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. You get a season plan, a session or a parent email in seconds.

WHAT'S INSIDE (10 chapters, 15 prompts each)
- Season planning and periodization
- Sprints and hurdles sessions
- Middle and long distance sessions
- Jumps (long, triple, high, pole vault)
- Throws (shot, discus, javelin, hammer)
- Warm-ups, drills and injury prevention
- Youth athletics and large group management
- Meet preparation and race strategy
- Communication, motivation and mental skills
- Club admin: parents, recruitment, social media, fundraising

WHO IT'S FOR
High school and club coaches, volunteer parent coaches, youth athletics leaders and athletes who coach themselves.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Download the PDF instantly after purchase.
2. Pick a prompt and replace the [brackets] with your details.
3. Paste it into any AI assistant (free versions work).
